WHY BYTE BY BYTE

Learning beyond the textbook.

I created Byte by Byte — Learning from Tech Minds because I wanted to learn directly from people working at the forefront of technology. Each conversation gives me—and other students—a chance to hear the real stories behind technology careers: the choices, challenges, lessons, and ideas that rarely fit inside a textbook.

By speaking with executives, researchers, professors, engineers, and entrepreneurs, I’m developing real-world perspective, communication and critical-thinking skills, and a growing network of mentors and leaders.

Most importantly, I hope the podcast makes complex areas of technology feel more approachable and encourages other students to stay curious, ask better questions, and imagine where their own interests could take them.
